Assumptions & economic guardrails

All local depot weights, 7-day opening stock age, and uneven SKU demand are synthetic demonstration assumptions. The 4x signal is case fact; the all-depot uplift is a stress interpretation. There is no outlet-level GT data, recipe bill-of-materials, spare cold-chain capacity, or actual service history. Buyer-age exposure is a screening proxy, not actual ₹6 crore loss.

Case-wide non-additive costs: ₹6 crore write-offs/discounts, ₹3.2 crore air freight paid (illustrative incremental ₹2.56 crore at 5x normal). Inter-depot moves cost ₹1.50/bottle; 2–4 days, human-validated expiry.
